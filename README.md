### Hey, I'm Koushith 👋

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at **[Consuma](https://consuma.ai)**, a rapid consumer research platform that turns millions of public conversations into evidence-backed reports in minutes. I lead engineering on its agentic AI platform, from the agent runtime and evaluation pipeline to the data infrastructure underneath, and I run enterprise go-lives with client teams from scoping through launch. Before this I was on the founding team at **[Reclaim Protocol](https://reclaimprotocol.org)** (YC W21), where I led integrations and built the SDKs and developer platform partners ship on.

I like software that respects the people who use it: local-first, private by default, and pleasant to look at. Most of my time goes to the seam where design meets engineering, turning rough ideas into products that feel obvious in hindsight.

I'm stack-agnostic. I reach for whatever language or framework fits the problem, and pick up whatever I'm missing along the way. The product matters more than the tool.

#### 🚀 Currently building

### [SideNotes](https://sidenotes.me) · a calm, local-first second brain

A markdown notes app that stays out of your way. Your notes are plain files on your own disk, never locked in a cloud. It comes with voice dictation, built-in Excalidraw sketches, live Mermaid diagrams, one-click Git backup, and an optional AI assistant you point at your own model.
